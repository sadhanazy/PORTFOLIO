A little context about how I approach cybersecurity — beyond a list of tools and credentials.

I’m a cybersecurity practitioner focused on understanding what security events are really telling us.

I enjoy working through the investigation process: collecting and analysing logs, connecting activity across endpoints, identifying indicators of compromise, and turning findings into clear escalation notes. I’m especially interested in the practical side of SOC operations and how well-tuned detections can help analysts respond with confidence.

My hands-on work spans Wazuh and Splunk labs, Python-based threat-intelligence enrichment, and packet-level investigation with Wireshark. I keep building, testing and documenting so that each lab becomes a repeatable learning resource.
